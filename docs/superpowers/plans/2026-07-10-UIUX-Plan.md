**The Aesthetic & Design System:**
1.  **Vibe:** Minimalist, sleek, spacious, and highly interactive.
2.  **Surfaces & Containers:** Use subtle glassmorphism (backdrop blur, low-opacity borders), soft drop shadows (`shadow-sm`, `shadow-md` on hover), and rounded corners (`rounded-xl` or `rounded-2xl`).
3.  **Micro-interactions:** Everything should feel alive. Hover states on buttons/cards, smooth transitions for tabs, and animated mounts/unmounts for collapsing sections.
4.  **Typography:** Clean, legible, with strong hierarchical contrast (muted grays for helper text, crisp darks/whites for headings).
